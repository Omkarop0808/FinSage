## 🎤 Detailed Script & Director's Cues

### 🎬 Scene 1: The Hook & Introduction
* **Timeline:** `0:00 - 0:20`
* **Visual Cue:** Start with a full-screen screen recording of the **FinSage** landing page. Hover smoothly over the navigation bar (highlighting the new **FinSage** branding) and scroll down slowly through the responsive, beautiful landing sections.
* **Narration:**
  > *"As college students and young professionals, managing money is a massive headache. Spreadsheet formulas are tedious, budgeting apps feel like chores, and financial advice online is scattered and generic.*
  >
  > *Meet **FinSage**—the modern, dark-mode, multi-agent AI co-pilot designed to democratize financial literacy and give you complete control of your financial destiny."*

---

### 🎬 Scene 2: Frictionless Onboarding
* **Timeline:** `0:20 - 0:35`
* **Visual Cue:** Click the premium, glowing **"Start Your Journey"** or **"Get Started Free"** button. The screen shifts with a smooth transition to the `Auth` page. Click **"Sign in with Google"**. Show a brief loading spinner, then redirect directly into the dashboard with a clean fade-in.
* **Narration:**
  > *"Getting started is instant. With secure Google authentication, FinSage automatically initializes your personal profile, setting up your workspace in seconds. No complicated forms, just direct access to your financial cockpit."*

---

### 🎬 Scene 3: The Main Financial Cockpit
* **Timeline:** `0:35 - 1:00`
* **Visual Cue:** Navigate the **Dashboard** page. Move the cursor smoothly over the interactive charts showing expense breakdowns, monthly income cards, and spending categories. Hover over dynamic charts to show tooltips popping up with premium micro-animations.
* **Narration:**
  > *"Welcome to the main dashboard. Here, your money is visualized beautifully. You get a clear, consolidated view of your income, real-time spending habits, and asset distribution.*
  >
  > *Every transaction is logged and categorized dynamically, giving you visual feedback on exactly where your money is going."*

---

### 🎬 Scene 4: Goal Plans & Custom Recommendations
* **Timeline:** `1:00 - 1:25`
* **Visual Cue:** Click on **"Goal Plans"** in the sidebar. Click the **"Create a Goal"** button to open the modal. Click inside the **"Deadline"** field—show how the native calendar selector pops up instantly (using the updated `showPicker` interaction). Type in a test goal and select a date. 
* **Next Visual:** Navigate to the **"Stock Recommendations"** page. Show the toggles for risk profiles (Low, Medium, High risk) and the resulting curated investment guides.
* **Narration:**
  > *"Planning for the future is just as easy. Simply set your saving goals and pick a target date. With our newly optimized calendar picker, scheduling your milestones is seamless.*
  >
  > *Ready to invest but don't know where to start? FinSage analyzes your unique risk appetite—whether conservative or aggressive—to give you tailored stock suggestions and simple, beginner-friendly investment guidelines."*

---

### 🎬 Scene 5: Frictionless WhatsApp Bot Logging
* **Timeline:** `1:25 - 1:55`
* **Visual Cue:** Navigate to the **WhatsApp Linking Page** inside the web app to show the linking instructions. Next, transition to a screen showing your mobile phone screen or a WhatsApp web chat. 
* **Interaction:** Type: `expense, food, 350, Dinner with friends` and press send. Show the Twilio bot responding immediately with a confirmation message branded with **FinSage**. Open the web app transactions list and hit refresh to show the new expense instantly appearing at the top of the ledger.
* **Narration:**
  > *"But the ultimate friction in budgeting is manual entry. That's why FinSage integrates directly with your **WhatsApp**.*
  >
  > *Simply text your transactions in plain text to the secure FinSage number—like 'expense, food, 350, Dinner with friends'—and the AI instantly logs, categorizes, and synchronizes it to your account. No app opening required. It is budgeting at the speed of thought."*

---

### 🎬 Scene 6: The AI Council Synthesis (The Climax)
* **Timeline:** `1:55 - 2:20`
* **Visual Cue:** Navigate to the **Council Synthesis** tab. Click **"Run Synthesis"** or display the pre-rendered active video briefing. Point the cursor to the four distinct agent profiles: **Budgeting Agent**, **Savings Agent**, **Debt Agent**, and **Investment Agent**. Highlight the consolidated plan summary below it.
* **Narration:**
  > *"Traditional apps just give you numbers. FinSage gives you a strategy. Our signature **AI Council** brings together four specialized AI agents.*
  >
  > *Instead of giving you conflicting advice, the Budgeting, Savings, Debt, and Investment agents debate behind the scenes, reconcile your balances, and synthesize a single, unified monthly video briefing and action plan. It's like having a team of elite financial advisors working for you for free."*

* **Timeline:** `2:20 - 2:30`
* **Visual Cue:** Show a quick shot of the Expo mobile app running on a mobile simulator or phone (utilizing Tunnel mode), rendering identical data. Transition back to the beautiful landing page with the glowing title **FinSage**.
* **Narration:**
  > *"With full cross-sync to our Expo mobile app, financial confidence is always in your pocket.*
  >
  > *Stop stressing. Start growing. Take control of your money today with **FinSage**!"*

## 💡 Pro-Tips for Recording the Demo
1. **Resolution:** Record in standard 1080p (1920x1080) for crisp text.
2. **Audio:** Use a high-quality USB microphone in a quiet room to make your narration voice premium and professional.
3. **Cursor Size:** Increase your system mouse pointer size slightly or enable a circular pointer highlight so viewers can easily track where you click.
4. **Transition Timing:** Practice clicking the sidebar links in sync with your speech to ensure the pacing feels natural and fluid.
